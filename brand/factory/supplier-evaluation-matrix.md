## Decision Checklist (Per Supplier)

Before signing with any supplier, confirm:

- [ ] Visited facility or received video tour
- [ ] Received and approved test samples
- [ ] Reviewed and accepted COA documentation
- [ ] Confirmed MOQ and pricing in writing
- [ ] Confirmed lead times in writing
- [ ] Reviewed their client references
- [ ] Agreed on IP / formulation ownership terms
- [ ] Confirmed packaging material compatibility
- [ ] Confirmed export / shipping logistics
- [ ] Signed NDA if sharing proprietary formulas

---

## Red Flags — Immediate Disqualification

- No GMP or equivalent certification
- Cannot provide COA for test batches
- MOQ exceeds 5000 units with no pilot option
- Cannot produce custom colors/formats
- Stock formulas only, no customization
- Lead time > 16 weeks
- No written communication trail (phone-only)
- Refuses to sign NDA
- No experience with food/supplement category

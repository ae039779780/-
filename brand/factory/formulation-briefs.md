# DAY//AFTR — Formulation Briefs

## Purpose

These briefs are shared with contract manufacturers to guide formula development. They describe the desired functional profile, ingredient direction, and sensory targets for each SKU.

---

## RESET — Recovery Powder Formula Brief

### Product Overview
- **SKU:** RESET
- **Format:** Powder (sachet + tub)
- **Category:** Electrolyte / recovery drink mix
- **Serving Size:** 1 sachet (~8-12g) in 250ml cold water

### Functional Profile
The formula should support:
1. Rapid rehydration via electrolyte replenishment
2. Mineral support (magnesium-forward)
3. Light cognitive support / clarity
4. General recovery from physical exertion, heat, travel, sleep debt

### Ingredient Direction

**Core Electrolyte Matrix:**
- Sodium (300-600mg per serving)
- Potassium (200-400mg per serving)
- Magnesium glycinate or citrate (150-300mg per serving)
- Calcium (50-100mg per serving)

**Recovery Support:**
- NAC (N-Acetyl Cysteine) — 300-600mg
- DHM (Dihydromyricetin) — 150-300mg
- L-Theanine — 100-200mg

**Vitamin Support (light):**
- B-Complex (B1, B6, B12 at 50-100% RDI)
- Vitamin C — 100-250mg

**Optional Botanical:**
- Wheat germ extract
- Light greens blend (spirulina, chlorella — small dose for color support)

### Sensory Targets
- **Color:** Electric blue (food-grade color system — spirulina + blue butterfly pea or approved blue colorant)
- **Taste:** Clean, slightly citrus-forward, not sweet. No artificial sweetener aftertaste.
- **Sweetener:** Stevia leaf extract or monk fruit — minimal. Target mild sweetness only.
- **Texture:** Fully dissolved, no grit, no foam
- **Aroma:** Light citrus, clean

### Flavor Variants
1. **Blue Citrus** — lemon + lime + hint of grapefruit
2. **Arctic Berry** — blueberry + acai + light berry
3. **Midnight Lemon** — pure lemon + hint of ginger

### What to Avoid
- Iron supplementation (not suitable for general recovery positioning)
- 5-HTP (regulatory complexity)
- Overstuffed superfood blends (keep formula focused)
- High-dose caffeine or stimulants
- Artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives
- Sugar or high-calorie sweeteners

### Regulatory Notes
- Position as food supplement / dietary supplement
- Avoid therapeutic claims on label
- Support claims only: "Hydration + Mineral Support"
- All ingredients must be GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe)
- Comply with Israeli Ministry of Health supplement regulations

## RITUAL — Botanical Tea Formula Brief

### Product Overview
- **SKU:** RITUAL
- **Format:** Pyramid tea sachets, individually wrapped
- **Category:** Premium botanical tea
- **Serving Size:** 1 sachet in ~200ml hot water, steep 4-5 minutes

### Functional Profile
1. Nervous system calming / downregulation
2. Gentle digestive support
3. Relaxation without sedation
4. Ritual / mindfulness anchor

### Blend Direction (4 variants)

**Botanical Blue:**
- Blue butterfly pea flower (color + mild antioxidant)
- Chamomile
- Lavender
- Hint of vanilla

**Cold Citrus Mint:**
- Peppermint
- Lemon verbena
- Citrus peel (lemon + orange)
- Hint of ginger

**Night Bloom:**
- Passionflower
- Valerian root (light dose)
- Chamomile
- Rose petals
- Lemon balm

**Morning Glass:**
- Green tea (light caffeine, ~20mg)
- Ginger root
- Turmeric
- Black pepper (bioavailability)
- Lemongrass

### Sensory Targets
- **Visual:** Beautiful in glass — colors should be visible and appealing
- **Aroma:** Prominent, aromatic, premium-feeling
- **Taste:** Smooth, balanced, no bitterness. Each blend should have distinct character.
- **Sachet:** Pyramid shape, biodegradable material preferred

### What to Avoid
- Synthetic flavorings
- Excessive filler herbs
- Medicinal/pharmaceutical taste
- Dust or fine particles (premium whole-leaf preferred)

## PRALINES — Functional Chocolate Formula Brief

### Product Overview
- **SKU:** PRALINES
- **Format:** Individual chocolates in gift box (6pc or 12pc)
- **Category:** Functional confectionery / luxury chocolates
- **Weight:** ~12-15g per praline

### Functional Profile
1. Adaptogen delivery (subtle, not medicinal)
2. Mood/relaxation support
3. Premium taste experience first, function second
4. Giftable — taste must stand on its own

### Flavor + Functional Direction (5 variants)

**Dark Cacao + Blueberry:**
- 72% dark chocolate shell
- Blueberry ganache center
- Functional: Ashwagandha extract (50-100mg)

**Citrus Shell:**
- 65% dark chocolate
- Orange/yuzu infused ganache
- Functional: Reishi mushroom extract (50-100mg)

**Mint Cacao:**
- 72% dark chocolate
- Peppermint ganache
- Functional: L-Theanine (50-100mg)

**Espresso Dark:**
- 80% dark chocolate
- Espresso cream center
- Functional: Lion's Mane extract (50-100mg)

**Yuzu Cacao:**
- 65% dark chocolate
- Yuzu curd center
- Functional: Magnesium glycinate (50mg)

### Sensory Targets
- **Primary:** Premium chocolate experience — must compete with luxury chocolatiers
- **Texture:** Smooth shell snap, creamy center
- **Functional ingredients should be undetectable in taste**
- **No supplement aftertaste**

### What to Avoid
- Visible supplement particles in chocolate
- Chalky or powdery texture
- Overpowering functional ingredient taste
- Cheap chocolate base (<60% cacao)
- Artificial emulsifiers or preservatives where possible

### Regulatory Notes
- Position as luxury confectionery, not supplement
- Functional ingredients at "food-level" doses
- No health claims on packaging exterior
- Interior card can mention ingredient benefits in lifestyle context
- Check local regulations on adaptogen dosing in food products

# DAY//AFTR — Production Timeline & Milestones

## Overview

This document outlines the production timeline from formulation to first commercial shipment for all DAY//AFTR SKUs.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1-3)

### Month 1: Supplier Selection & Sampling
| Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Send outreach emails to 10-15 manufacturers per SKU | Asaf | Responses + initial quotes |
| Request samples from top 3 per category | Asaf | Sample kits received |
| Score suppliers on internal scorecard | Asaf | Ranked supplier list |
| Begin formulation discussions (RESET) | Formulator | Draft formula brief |
| Engage packaging designer | Asaf | Design brief signed off |

### Month 2: Formulation Development
| Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------------|
| RESET: 3 formula iterations with chosen manufacturer | Formulator | Test batches |
| RITUAL: Source botanical blend suppliers | Tea manufacturer | Sample sachets |
| PRALINES: Flavor development (5 flavors) | Chocolatier | Tasting samples |
| Packaging: First design concepts (all SKUs) | Designer | Mockup presentations |
| Regulatory review: Label claims compliance | Regulatory consultant | Compliance report |

### Month 3: Formula Finalization
| Task | Owner | Deliverable |
|------|-------|-------------|
| RESET: Final formula sign-off | Asaf + Formulator | Locked formula |
| RITUAL: Final blend ratios confirmed | Tea manufacturer | Locked blends x4 |
| PRALINES: Final flavor profiles locked | Chocolatier | Locked recipes x5 |
| Packaging: Final designs approved | Designer + Asaf | Print-ready files |
| Nutrition facts / ingredient panels finalized | Regulatory | Final label copy |

## Key Supplier Requirements

### Minimum Qualifications
- GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) certified
- ISO 22000 or equivalent food safety certification
- HACCP compliant
- Export license (if manufacturing outside Israel)
- Capacity for MOQ of 500-5000 units per SKU
- Custom formulation support
- Premium packaging capability (foil stamping, magnetic closures, matte finishes)

### Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with premium/luxury wellness brands
- In-house testing lab with COA capability
- Design-sensitive communication style
- Previous export to EU/US markets
- Ability to produce color-matched formulas (electric blue for RESET)

## Risk Register

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Formula color instability (RESET blue) | Medium | High | Test 3+ color systems, stability testing early |
| Packaging delays (custom magnetic boxes) | High | Medium | Order materials 8 weeks ahead, have backup supplier |
| MOQ too high for launch volume | Medium | High | Negotiate pilot pricing, consider co-manufacturing |
| Regulatory claims rejection | Low | High | Pre-review with regulatory consultant before print |
| Shelf life shorter than expected | Low | High | Accelerated stability testing in Month 4 |
| Supplier communication gaps | Medium | Medium | Weekly check-ins, written briefs for all decisions |

# Supplier Outreach Template

## Subject Line
Private Label Inquiry — Premium Wellness Brand / [Powder / Tea / Pralines]

## Email Body

Hello,

My name is Asaf, and I am building a premium wellness brand called DAY//AFTR. We are sourcing manufacturing partners for:

- **Powder drink mix** (electrolyte recovery formula)
- **Premium botanical tea sachets** (pyramid sachet format)
- **Luxury pralines / functional chocolate bites** (gift box format)

We are looking for:
- Private label or contract manufacturing capability
- Premium packaging support
- Strong QA documentation
- Export readiness

Please confirm the following:
1. Product formats supported
2. Minimum order quantity (MOQ)
3. Lead times for pilot and production runs
4. Certifications held (GMP, ISO, HACCP, etc.)
5. Sampling options and cost
6. Custom formulation support
7. Packaging capabilities (custom molds, premium finishes)
8. COA / testing process

We are a design-sensitive brand with high packaging standards. Please share any relevant catalogs, certifications, or case studies.

Best regards,
Asaf
DAY//AFTR

## Internal Scorecard Criteria

### Manufacturing Fit
- Supports requested format
- Premium positioning fit
- Custom development support

### Commercial Fit
- MOQ suitable for pilot
- Unit economics reasonable
- Lead times acceptable

### Quality Fit
- Strong certifications
- Clear QA documentation
- Credible testing process

### Brand Fit
- Premium packaging capability
- Good communication
- Understands design-sensitive brands

### Red Flags
- Generic answers only
- No clear testing process
- Weak packaging support
- Stock formulas only
- Slow or vague communication
- No export experience

# DAY//AFTR — Comprehensive Marketing Strategy

## Brand Essence

**Mission:** Help high-performers return to baseline faster, cleaner, and more consistently.

**Brand Voice:** Controlled. Clinical. Luxury. Slightly nocturnal. Never bro-science, never preachy.

**Core Promise:** A premium recovery system — not a hangover cure, not a detox, not a supplement. A system.

**Tagline:** "Recovery System for Demanding Lives"

---

## Target Audience

### Primary: The High-Output Professional (25-40)
- Tech founders, creatives, consultants, finance professionals
- Travel frequently (2+ flights/month)
- Social calendar demands recovery
- Values design, aesthetics, premium quality
- Willing to pay premium for things that work and look good
- Active on Instagram, LinkedIn, occasionally TikTok

### Secondary: The Design-Conscious Gifter (28-45)
- Buys premium gifts for partners, colleagues, friends
- Drawn to packaging and unboxing experiences
- Shops at Aesop, Le Labo, specialty stores
- PRALINES + RITUAL are entry points

### Tertiary: The Wellness-Adjacent Biohacker (22-35)
- Tracks health metrics, optimizes routines
- Familiar with AG1, Athletic Greens, LMNT
- Interested in the BASE + app ecosystem
- Engages with content about formulation and ingredients

## Channel Strategy

### Instagram (@dayaftr)
**Role:** Primary brand-building channel
**Posting:** 4-5x/week
**Content Mix:**
- 3 Reels/week (recovery lifestyle + unboxing + science)
- 1-2 carousel/static posts (product info, ingredient spotlights)
- Daily Stories (behind-the-scenes, polls, waitlist updates)
- Monthly Lives (founder Q&A, product reveals)

**Aesthetic:** Dark feed, consistent matte black + electric blue palette. No bright colors, no nature imagery. Clean grid layout.

### TikTok (@dayaftr)
**Role:** Discovery and virality
**Posting:** 3-4x/week
**Content Mix:**
- "Morning after" POV videos
- ASMR unboxing (PRALINES, RITUAL)
- Quick recovery tips
- "What's in my RESET" ingredient breakdowns
- Creator partnerships

### LinkedIn (DAY//AFTR)
**Role:** B2B credibility + premium audience targeting
**Posting:** 2-3x/week
**Content Mix:**
- Founder journey posts
- Product development insights
- Wellness industry commentary
- "Building a premium brand in 2026" content
- Corporate gifting angle for PRALINES

### Email / Newsletter
**Role:** Conversion + retention
**Cadence:** Weekly during pre-launch, 2x/week during launch
**Segments:**
1. Waitlist (nurture → convert)
2. Early access (exclusive drops)
3. Customers (retention, upsell, referral)
4. Gift purchasers (seasonal reminders)

**Key Sequences:**
- Welcome sequence (5 emails over 10 days)
- Pre-launch countdown (7-day sequence)
- Post-purchase (thank you → review request → referral)
- Re-engagement (30-day inactive)
- Subscription nudge (for RESET repeat buyers)

## Launch Sequence

### Stage 0: Stealth / Brand Building (Months 1-4)
- Build Instagram presence (aesthetic, mood, teaser content)
- Grow waitlist through landing page
- Founder content on LinkedIn
- No product reveals yet — just brand mood
- Target: 2,000 waitlist signups

### Stage 1: Reveal (Month 5)
- Product reveals: RESET + PRALINES
- Full website launch with store
- First unboxing content from creators
- Email blast to waitlist: "Early access opens in 2 weeks"
- Limited PR outreach to 5-10 premium publications

### Stage 2: Early Access Drop (Month 6)
- Waitlist gets 48-hour exclusive access
- Launch Combo bundle (RESET + PRALINES) at 12% off
- First 200 orders get hand-signed founder's note
- Real-time stock counter on website
- Social proof: unboxing UGC from early access customers

### Stage 3: Public Launch (Month 7)
- Open store to all
- Paid advertising begins (Instagram, Facebook, Google)
- RITUAL tea launch (adds lifestyle depth)
- Full creator partnership program activated
- PR push: design/lifestyle publications

### Stage 4: Growth (Months 8-12)
- Subscription model for RESET
- Corporate gifting program (PRALINES)
- Seasonal limited editions
- BASE capsule pre-orders
- App beta waitlist
- International shipping exploration

## Influencer & Creator Strategy

### Tier 1: Macro Creators (100K-1M followers) — 3-5 partnerships
- Premium lifestyle, design, or wellness creators
- Long-form review + unboxing content
- Fee: Product + $1,000-5,000 per post
- Target: Instagram Reels, YouTube

### Tier 2: Mid-Tier Creators (10K-100K) — 10-20 partnerships
- Niche wellness, biohacking, design, travel creators
- Authentic integration (morning routine, travel kit)
- Fee: Product + $200-1,000 per post
- Target: Instagram, TikTok

### Tier 3: Micro/Nano (1K-10K) — 30-50 partnerships
- Local opinion leaders, aesthetic-focused accounts
- Gifted product (no fee) + affiliate code
- UGC rights for brand repurposing
- Target: Instagram Stories, TikTok

### Creator Brief Template
- Brand guidelines summary (do/don't)
- 3 key messages to convey
- Visual direction (dark bg, product-forward, no clutter)
- Required hashtags: #DAYAFTR #RecoverySystem
- Avoid mentioning: hangovers, drinking, substances

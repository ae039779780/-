# DAY//AFTR — Marketing Strategy

## Core Strategy
Sell the premium recovery lifestyle, not ingredient complexity. The hero is the system, not the formula.

## Positioning Angles (Approved)
- Long days and demanding routines
- Travel recovery
- Heat and dehydration support
- Low sleep / sleep debt
- High-output lifestyles
- Slow mornings and ritual recovery

## Positioning Angles (Avoid)
- Substance use or party recovery
- Harm reduction messaging
- Before/after substance use support
- Detox after substances

## Content Pillars

### 1. Recovery Lifestyle
- Reset ritual content
- Hydration habits
- Morning-after long days
- Travel routines
- Sleep debt recovery mindset

### 2. Luxury Gifting
- Premium tea set unboxing
- Praline presentation
- Gift editions and seasonal drops
- Founder aesthetic

### 3. Performance Ritual
- Baseline rebuild concept
- Readiness score (future app tie-in)
- Habit stacking
- Daily routine integration

## Funnel Strategy

### Top of Funnel
- Instagram / TikTok premium visual brand content
- Landing page waitlist
- Founder-led brand storytelling
- Luxury wellness UGC / creators

### Middle of Funnel
- Product comparison visuals
- Ritual-based education
- Bundle positioning
- Email sequences

### Bottom of Funnel
- Waitlist conversion
- Limited first drop
- Gift set offer
- Subscription upsell

## Launch Sequence

### Stage 1: RESET + PRALINES
- Functional clarity (RESET) + differentiation and desirability (PRALINES)
- Sharpest first commercial combination

### Stage 2: Add RITUAL Tea
- Deepens the brand story
- Lifestyle extension

### Stage 3: Add BASE Capsules + App Beta
- Daily retention through capsules
- App increases subscription stickiness

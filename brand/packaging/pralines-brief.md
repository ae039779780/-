# PRALINES — Packaging Brief

## Product Format
Functional luxury pralines — 6-piece or 12-piece gift box

## Material Direction
- Rigid drawer box or magnetic gift box
- Premium insert tray with numbered cavities
- Midnight blue, black, and silver palette
- Matte exterior with optional spot UV or foil

## Visual Identity
- Gifting-first design — must look like a luxury chocolate box
- Dark, refined aesthetic with cold blue accents
- Numbered praline cavities for a curated feel
- No health/supplement visual cues on exterior

## Typography on Pack
- **Brand**: DAY//AFTR — uppercase, tracked-out
- **Product**: PRALINES — large, bold
- **Descriptor**: Luxury Ritual Chocolates
- **Support claim**: Functional Premium Bites

## Unboxing Experience
- Drawer pull or magnetic lid reveal
- Each praline seated in numbered cavity
- Flavor guide card with tasting notes
- Premium tissue paper or ribbon element

## References
- Luxury chocolatiers (Pierre Marcolini, La Maison du Chocolat)
- Premium gifting (Diptyque box design)
- Dark luxury packaging aesthetic

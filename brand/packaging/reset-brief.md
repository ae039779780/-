# RESET — Packaging Brief

## Product Format
Electric blue recovery powder — premium pouch or matte rigid tub

## Material Direction
- Soft-touch matte finish exterior
- Premium DTC-ready unboxing
- Silver foil details on logo and product name

## Visual Identity
- Electric blue glow accent (gradient or foil element)
- Minimal clinical layout
- Dark background (#060b16) with white and cyan typography
- No natural/organic visual clichés

## Typography on Pack
- **Brand**: DAY//AFTR — uppercase, tracked-out, top of pack
- **Product**: RESET — large, bold, geometric
- **Descriptor**: Electric Blue Recovery Powder
- **Support claim**: Hydration + Mineral Support
- **Secondary**: With Wheat Germ + Greens Blend

## Unboxing Experience
- Matte exterior with subtle texture
- Inner seal with brand mark
- Scoop included (if tub format)
- Instruction card with ritual guidance

## References
- Luxury skincare packaging (Aesop, Grown Alchemist)
- Clinical supplement aesthetic (AG1 premium tier)
- Boutique gifting feel

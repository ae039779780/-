# RITUAL — Packaging Brief

## Product Format
Premium pyramid tea sachets in rigid magnetic box

## Material Direction
- Rigid magnetic closure box
- Midnight blue interior lining
- Dark matte exterior
- Silver or white foil typography

## Visual Identity
- Dark, moody aesthetic — no bright colors
- Midnight navy and black palette with silver accents
- Boutique tea meets luxury beauty feel
- Individually wrapped sachets with brand mark

## Typography on Pack
- **Brand**: DAY//AFTR — uppercase, tracked-out
- **Product**: RITUAL — large, bold
- **Descriptor**: Botanical Tea Collection
- **Support claim**: Refined Reset Tea
- **Secondary**: Premium Sachets

## Unboxing Experience
- Magnetic lid reveal
- Dark interior with sachets nested in rows
- Each sachet individually wrapped in branded paper
- Flavor guide card included

## References
- Premium tea brands (Paper & Tea, Palais des Thés)
- Luxury beauty gift sets
- Dark minimalist packaging
